# Negative results

1. **Mass leak** — unbalanced cleavage stoichiometry cannot obtain elemental
   carbon conservation; receiver exits nonzero.
2. **Forged trajectory** — states that preserve a numeric pool but are not exact
   fires of `S` are rejected by `trajectory_conservation`.
3. **False refutation** — attempting to refute a true conservation law fails.
4. **Producer PASS** — embedding `verdict: PASS` causes hard reject.

These are intentional fail-closed behaviors, not defects.
