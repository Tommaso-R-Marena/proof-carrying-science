How the model works

Across each edge, transfer Δt × conductance × (left concentration − right concentration). Subtract that amount on the left, add it on the right, then divide each amount by its cell volume. All edges read the same previous row.

Mass is Σ volume × concentration. Energy here means Σ volume × concentration². A lower energy alone does not prove the update is correct.
