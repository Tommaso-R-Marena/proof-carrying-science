How the model works

Across each edge, transfer Δt × conductance × (left concentration − right concentration). Subtract that amount on the left, add it on the right, then divide each amount by its cell volume. All edges read the same previous row.

Mass is Σ volume × concentration. Energy here means Σ volume × concentration². A lower energy alone does not prove the update is correct.

Counterexample workshop

Build a convincing impostor

Start with the exact model trace. Move an amount between any two cells in its final row. Can you keep the summaries green while breaking the update rule?

Use canonical integers or fractions, such as 0, 1/32 or -1/8. Positive amounts move from the first cell to the second. Negative amounts reverse direction.

The interval keeps mass, bounds, and energy no greater than the model's final energy. It is stricter than the earlier energy summary. Its mathematical formula is proved separately in Lean; these browser calculations are tested implementations. Export a case to check its actual trace with Lean.
