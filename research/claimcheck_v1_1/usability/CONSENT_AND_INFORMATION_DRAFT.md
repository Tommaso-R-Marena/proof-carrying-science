**Draft. To be replaced by the institution's template and approved before any use.**

## What this is

We are testing a research tool, ClaimCheck, which checks whether a number printed in a paper can be
recomputed from a data file. We are testing the tool, not you.

## What you would do

About 40 minutes, one session: use the tool on example data we provide, think aloud, and answer a
few questions about what the tool showed. You can stop at any time without giving a reason.

## Your data

We record: whether each task was completed, your answers, the time per task, your comments, your
field and career stage. No audio or video unless you agree separately in writing. No other personal
data. Please do not upload any data of your own. Records are kept by [holder] at [place] for [period]
and then deleted. You can ask for your records to be withdrawn until [date]. Results are reported
without your name, unless you ask to be named.
