## Integration meaning

Conditional checker success can establish that an optimized guard equals an
original guard under explicit satisfiable premises. Combined with a separately
kernel-checked invariant of the original guard, candidate guard acceptance
preserves that invariant. This is useful executable assurance when receiving
replay binds exact task/source/assumptions and rejects unresolved outcomes. It
cannot infer global AI safety, empirical adequacy or deployed-world behavior
from conditional Boolean equivalence alone.

Protected qualification must use the existing standard free runner with its
bounded swap/resource setup. `--memory-mb` is capped at 32000 and
`--heavy-timeout` at 3600 seconds. Sequential build and replay fail closed on
any compiler error, timeout, dropped inventory, source change or nonapproved axiom.
Higher resources are not proof evidence until every exact command actually succeeds.
