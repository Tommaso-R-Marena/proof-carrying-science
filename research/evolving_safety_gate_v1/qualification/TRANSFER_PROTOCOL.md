# Matched observation study

Frozen before scoring. Evaluate the seed detectors from commit 5cd0fe136bef17b2b869e6df96697aae256c1fe6. No model, training, fitted threshold or attack content enters this study.

Two toy workflows route books to shelves and museum objects to trays. Each has two items, two permitted routes per workflow (one per item), two possible proposals per item, and two stylistic notes crossed with all proposals. The controller executes the mandated route regardless of the proposal. This gives 16 equiprobable episodes. The target is whether the proposed route would have landed the item in an incorrect destination. The generated rows carry no attack flags or labels. The adjudicator reads the final location in a hypothetical uncorrected ledger against an enumerated approved inventory.

Compute detection counts for the frozen seed detectors on a projection with completion, executed route and raw proposal/note. Omit all precomputed harm flags. Missing inputs are part of the transfer condition. Compare directly checking the declared policy against the proposal. Measure the exact minimum number of classification errors achievable when only the executed move is visible and when both the move and proposed destination are visible. Allow abstention only on entire observation classes. This is a census of synthetic policy exceptions, not a population sample. Coverage is discrete and no sampling confidence interval applies. Designers knew the frozen detectors. An external evaluator and future deployment prevalence remain unmeasured.

Run `python research/evolving_safety_gate_v1/qualification/replay_transfer.py` at checkout root. The receiver loads the two original detector modules from the pinned Git commit in a fresh directory. The composed candidate here is its offline catalog detector. The full external classifier stack has a different interface.

All five seed detectors missed all eight incorrect proposals with zero false alarms. Checking the proposed route against the enforced move recovered all eight with zero false alarms. At full coverage, every observer restricted to the executed move must make at least eight mistakes. The bound falls to zero when the proposed destination is visible. The minimum count is calculated independently of the frozen detectors by grouping indistinguishable rows, and checked against enumeration of all deterministic selective rules on small fixtures. This example measures the need for policy-specific interpretation of telemetry, even where a richer observation would suffice in principle.

## Live policy-reading control

A subsequent Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite run received the explicit routing policy and the 16 proposals and executions. The sampled response labeled 16/16 proposals correctly with zero abstentions. This is a different information condition from the historical detectors, which lack this workflow's policy. The deterministic route comparator also achieved 16/16.
