Make critical scientific computation independently verifiable.

Proof-Carrying Science turns bounded computational workflows into replayable evidence packages: what was claimed, what assumptions were made, what artifacts were used, what checks were independently replayed, and what remains unresolved.

Explicit claims

Scientific assertions are represented as scoped claims with machine-readable predicates and named assumptions.

Replayable evidence

The verifier reruns supported checks from packaged artifacts instead of trusting a recorded PASS value.

Signed artifacts

Certificates can be Ed25519-signed, bundled deterministically, archived, and independently checked.

Honest boundaries

Computational correctness is kept separate from empirical, biological, clinical, and regulatory validation.

Initial focus: computational biopharma

The first restricted adapter covers one-compartment IV-bolus PK with a direct Emax PD layer, dimensional contracts, provenance, and independent equation replay.

What a PCS certificate does not mean

A passing computational certificate does not establish that a biological model is adequate for a drug, that a therapy is safe or effective, that a workflow is regulator-approved, or that unsupported arbitrary code is correct.
