Private design-partner alpha

Make critical scientific computation independently verifiable.

Proof-Carrying Science turns a bounded computational workflow into a replayable evidence package: exact artifacts, explicit assumptions, checks or proofs, runtime provenance, signer identity, reviewer policy, and what remains unresolved.

Scoped claims

Consequential assertions are represented explicitly, with named assumptions and machine-readable predicates where supported.

Replayable evidence

The verifier reruns supported checks from packaged artifacts instead of trusting a producer-recorded PASS value.

Authenticated handoff

A signed package manifest binds the certificate, human-readable report, limitations, runtime provenance, and delivered artifacts.

Reviewer-controlled acceptance

The producer does not choose the final acceptance threshold. Reviewers can supply an external policy and pin the expected signer fingerprint.

Four questions, kept separate

Did the evidence replay?Scientific/computational replay status.
Is the package intact?All delivered files match the bound package manifest.
Who issued it?Signer authenticity is verified only against a trusted public key.
Does the reviewer accept it?An external policy decides whether the scoped result is acceptable for its context.

Initial focus: computational biopharma

The first restricted adapter covers one-compartment IV-bolus PK with a direct Emax PD layer, dimensional contracts, provenance, and independent equation replay. The near-term product is a fixed-scope assurance pilot around a workflow the customer already cares about—not a claim to verify all of pharmacometrics.

Design-partner pilot

A partner supplies one bounded workflow and 3–10 reviewer-defined claims. PCS returns a signed evidence package that an independent reviewer can replay from scratch, plus an explicit limitations statement and runtime provenance.

What a PCS certificate does not mean

A passing computational certificate does not establish that a biological model is adequate for a drug, that a therapy is safe or effective, that a workflow is regulator-approved or GxP validated, or that unsupported arbitrary code is correct.

Proof-Carrying Science — private research and engineering preview. The current executable replay checker is Python. The Lean assurance kernel and Python-to-Lean refinement are active research work and are not described as machine-checked until a real Lean build succeeds.
