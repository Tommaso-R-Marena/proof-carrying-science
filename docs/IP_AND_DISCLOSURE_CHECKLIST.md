# IP and Disclosure Checklist

This is an operational checklist, not legal advice.

Before importing research into the company or publishing it, record:

- project/repository;
- contributors and likely inventors;
- dates of conception/reduction to practice;
- university/lab affiliation at time of work;
- grant/sponsor support;
- datasets and their licenses/DUAs;
- compute/equipment/facilities used;
- employment/consulting agreements;
- existing public disclosures/preprints/talks;
- third-party code/model licenses;
- patents/disclosures already filed;
- tech-transfer contact/status;
- company rights needed (none / license / assignment / permission);
- publication clearance status.

## Architectural rule

Do **not** copy code from existing research repositories into the new commercial repository until provenance and rights are clear. Prefer adapters and published interfaces. This v0.1 package therefore reuses concepts rather than copying CertiForge/CASMI source.
