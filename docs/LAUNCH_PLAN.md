# Launch Plan — v0.4

## Launch principle

Do not wait for the universal system. Launch the smallest honest product whose assurance boundary is explicit.

The first commercial wedge is **verification and assurance of computational biopharma workflows**, beginning with restricted PK/PD workflows. The product verifies declared computational properties and packages replayable evidence. It does not claim that a model is biologically adequate, clinically valid, or regulator-approved.

## Four launch stages

### Stage 0 — Founder / research preview: now

Ready when:
- publication firewall is active;
- synthetic/public examples only;
- claims are carefully scoped;
- repository and demos are private unless intentionally released.

Goal: customer discovery and technical feedback, not revenue.

### Stage 1 — Private design-partner alpha: target 1–2 weeks

Required gates:
- valid evidence package can be generated and independently replayed;
- signed certificate flow works;
- deterministic evidence bundle works;
- PK/PD adapter has adversarial tests;
- one-page data-handling and security statement exists;
- pilot scope explicitly excludes clinical/regulatory certification;
- constituent-research IP/provenance has been reviewed enough to ensure the pilot uses only clean-room/public/synthetic material.

Deliverable: run PCS on one non-sensitive or synthetic workflow supplied by a design partner and produce an assurance report plus evidence bundle.

### Stage 2 — First paid pilot: target 3–6 weeks

Additional gates:
- business entity and payment/banking path established;
- attorney-reviewed pilot agreement / MSA + SOW or equivalent;
- confidentiality and data-handling terms;
- private repository access and least-privilege process;
- issue-response process;
- reproducible release/versioning policy;
- at least one external workflow has been successfully completed end-to-end.

The first engagement should be a **fixed-scope assurance pilot**, not a large enterprise software license.

### Stage 3 — Public commercial technical preview: target 8–12 weeks

Additional gates:
- public website and documentation;
- installation path that a third party can reproduce;
- at least 1–3 design partners or equivalent external validation;
- one publishable/public case study that does not compromise constituent research;
- compiled Lean assurance kernel or an explicit public disclosure that the Python checker remains the TCB;
- security threat model and signed release artifacts;
- support/contact process;
- clear open-core/commercial boundary.

## What should *not* block Stage 1 or Stage 2

- arbitrary Python verification;
- complete PK/PD coverage;
- GxP validation;
- regulator endorsement;
- all future domain packs;
- the full research institute;
- publication of every constituent project.

## What must block exaggerated claims

Do not market PCS as:
- proving a biological model true;
- proving clinical safety or efficacy;
- FDA validated/certified/approved;
- verifying arbitrary scientific Python;
- replacing empirical validation;
- providing universal formal verification across science.

## Initial measurable launch goals

1. 20–30 customer-discovery interviews.
2. 3 organizations willing to supply a representative workflow or specification.
3. 1 completed external design-partner assurance run.
4. 1 paid pilot proposal sent.
5. 1 independent technical reviewer successfully verifies a PCS bundle without founder assistance.
6. 0 known false accepts in the frozen launch adversarial suite.
