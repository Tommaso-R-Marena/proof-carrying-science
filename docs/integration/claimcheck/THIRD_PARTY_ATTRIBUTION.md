Each benchmark document is a published open-access article whose XML states CC BY 4.0, with its published data (CC0 1.0). The article text is used for research on checking reported statistics against data. Under CC BY the authors are credited below, the licence is linked, and changes are indicated:

* `article.xml` is the article as retrieved from Europe PMC (unchanged). No figures or images are included.
* `article.txt` and the `source.txt` copies in the replay bundles are a plain-text extraction (`acquire.jats_to_text`: title, abstract, body paragraphs and captions; tables, formula markup and references omitted).

* The data files are copies of the published files (byte-identical; SHA-256 recorded). CC0 requires no attribution; the source is credited anyway.
