# Founding Commercial Offer

## What we sell first

**Computational Assurance Pilot for Scientific Workflows**

A fixed-scope engagement that turns a bounded computational workflow into an independently replayable evidence package.

## Initial customer

Pharmacometrics, model-informed drug development, quantitative systems pharmacology, computational biology, or adjacent biopharma teams with custom code and a costly review burden.

## Customer brings

- one bounded workflow;
- 3–10 claims they need confidence in;
- approved artifacts or a synthetic equivalent;
- one technical reviewer.

## PCS delivers

- claim/assumption inventory;
- machine-readable assurance manifest;
- replayed computational checks;
- artifact hashes and provenance bindings;
- signed certificate;
- deterministic evidence bundle;
- human-readable report;
- limitations and unresolved obligations;
- one technical review session.

## What we explicitly do not sell yet

- regulatory certification;
- clinical validation;
- universal program correctness;
- GxP validation;
- automatic verification of arbitrary Python/R/C++;
- guarantees outside the agreed assurance boundary.

## Why a partner might buy this

The initial value hypothesis is not "formal methods are impressive." It is that high-stakes computational workflows are expensive to review, reproduce, transfer, and audit, and that a machine-replayable evidence object can reduce ambiguity and manual review while making limitations visible.
