# Publication Strategy

## Default rule

Publish substantive foundational discoveries whenever safety, privacy, contractual obligations, patent strategy, or third-party rights do not require a delay.

## Sequencing rule

Before public disclosure of potentially patentable inventions:

1. identify inventors/contributors;
2. identify university/lab/employer resource use and sponsorship;
3. determine contractual and technology-transfer obligations;
4. obtain appropriate IP/legal advice;
5. file/protect only if protection materially serves the mission;
6. then publish.

## Current-project stance

CertiForge, CASMI/QFD, Carnot/spectral geometry, AI-safety/formal work, and protein research should each be evaluated as research publications on their own scientific merits. The company should not require those results to become proprietary products merely because they may have commercial relevance.

Commercial products can instead provide deployment, checking, scale, integration, domain maintenance, and assurance services built on top of published principles.

## Publication pipeline

For each project maintain:

- claim ledger;
- evidence/reproduction status;
- adversarial tests;
- authorship/contribution ledger;
- IP/disclosure clearance status;
- target venue shortlist;
- preprint decision;
- reproducibility bundle;
- reviewer-response log.
