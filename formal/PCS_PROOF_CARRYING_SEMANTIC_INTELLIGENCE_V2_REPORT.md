# PCS Proof-Carrying Semantic Intelligence v2 — implementation report

This report covers the second layer built on top of **PCS Semantic Translation Contract v1**
(`PCS_SEMANTIC_TRANSLATION_V1_REPORT.md`). v1 is unchanged and still built, tested and
proved; v2 is a conservative extension of it (`v1_acceptance_implies_v2_contract`).

## 0. Scope and baseline

* Standalone Lake package `PCS` (version 0.5.0), toolchain `leanprover/lean4:v4.28.0`
  (unchanged, no Mathlib).
* The parent `RequestProject` workspace and the Python sources of the full repository
  (`proof_translation_v06.py`, Python Claim IR, Proof Obligation Graph, `pcs-proof-proposals-v1`)
  are **not** present. Integration that needs them remains OPEN (§9).
* No existing theorem, module name or import was removed or weakened. `PCS.lean` imports the
  new modules; `pcs-semantic-check` gained the `--v2`, `--v2-record` and
  `--check-countermodel` sub-commands (the v1 command line is unchanged).

## 1. What v2 adds

v1 accepts a candidate only if its canonical normal form *equals* the interpretation's
(up to α-renaming, `≠`-desugaring and assumption order). That is sound but rejects many
correct translations (`Q ∧ P` for `P ∧ Q`, `¬∃x,¬P x` for `∀x,P x`, …). v2 keeps every v1
gate and replaces only the semantic gate by a **proof-carrying** one:

    normal forms equal   ∨   ∃ cert, checkCert cert I.normalize C.normalize = true

and, when that fails, searches for a **verified countermodel**.

## 2. Main theorems (all kernel-checked; axioms ⊆ {`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`})

Semantic core
* `Rule.apply_sound`, `rewriteAt_sound`, `NClaim.applySteps_sound` — every rule, at every path
  (also under binders), preserves denotation in every model, valuation and environment.
* `checkCert_sound` / `translation_certificate_sound` /
  `certificate_acceptance_implies_semantic_preservation` — an accepted certificate implies
  `I.denote M ρ ↔ C.denote M ρ` for every model `M` and valuation `ρ`.
* `inequivalent_claims_admit_no_certificate`, `certificate_checker_rejects_invalid_derivations`.
* `untrusted_translation_search_cannot_forge_certified_equivalence` — for an *arbitrary*
  certificate generator, every certificate the checker accepts is sound.

Contract and classifier (`TranslationV2.lean`)
* `accepted_translation_preserves_denotation_v2` — `CertifiedContractV2 A req cert` implies
  `req.interpretation.selected.denote M ρ ↔ req.candidate.claim.denote M ρ` for **every**
  model and valuation.
* `decideV2_certified_iff` / `executable_semantic_checker_refines_specification` — the
  executable classifier outputs `CERTIFIED_TRANSLATION` exactly when all v2 gates hold and the
  semantic analysis links the claims; `decideV2_certified_complete` covers proposer-supplied
  certificates.
* `v1_acceptance_implies_v2_contract` — conservative extension.
* `decideV2_counterexample_sound` — `VERIFIED_COUNTEREXAMPLE` carries a re-checked witness;
  the claims are then not equivalent over registry-conforming models, v1 rejects, and no v2
  certificate exists. `countermodel_and_certificate_exclusive`.
* `decideV2_{ambiguity,unconfirmed,ungrounded,unsupported,missing_proof,inequivalent}_not_certified`.
* `semantic_authority_does_not_trust_proposer_metadata` — the decision is invariant under
  proposer identity, model confidence and the model's own "confirmed" flag.
* `pcs_proof_carrying_translation_sound_v2` — flagship: certified outcome + explicit
  `ExternalContracts` ⇒ no ambiguity, grounding, typing, closedness, fragment membership,
  hygiene, denotational equivalence, semantic link, authorized confirmation, elaboration and
  kernel-proof facts (when required), Explanation IR meaning, and — under the explicit
  `ElaborationBridge` — truth of the selected interpretation in the intended model.

Wire level (`TranslationV2Json.lean`)
* `semantic_wire_roundtrip`, `decRequestV2_encRequestV2` and per-type `dec*_enc*` lemmas.
* `semanticCheckV2_encRequestV2` — on canonical request bytes the front end computes `decideV2`.
* `semanticCheckV2_certified_sound`, `accepted_wire_translation_preserves_semantics` — a
  certified outcome on **raw bytes** implies the bytes decode to a request satisfying the v2
  contract, hence denotational equivalence.
* `malformed_wire_request_rejected` — undecodable input ⇒ `INVALID_PROPOSAL`.
* `semantic_authority_cannot_be_bypassed_by_unsigned_receipts` — certified ⇒ confirmation,
  elaboration and proof receipts exist, are bound to the exact interpretation / Lean source and
  verify under authorized keys of the right role.
* `countermodel_bundle_sound` — `VALID_COUNTERMODEL` ⇒ a registry-conforming finite model in
  which the two claims differ.

Finite models (`FiniteModel.lean`)
* `finite_evaluator_sound`, `finite_evaluator_complete_for_supported_finite_models`,
  `countermodel_witness_sound`, `countermodel_refutes_inhabited_equivalence`,
  `countermodel_excludes_certificate`, `search_found_sound`, `search_found_minimal`,
  `search_exhausted_complete` (exhaustion is complete only *within the bound*).

Explanation and CNL (`CNL.lean`, `TranslationV2.lean`)
* `controlled_language_print_parse`, `controlled_language_parse_print` (every accepted CNL
  sentence is the unique rendering of its formula), `controlled_language_render_preserves_semantics`,
  `renderCNL_injective`, `accepted_explanation_means_interpretation_v2`,
  `explanation_preserves_{quantifier_scope,assumptions,grounded_definitions,proof_limitations}`.

Composition, repair, caching, obligation graphs (`SemanticCompose.lean`)
* `SemEquiv.*_congr`, `compositional_translation_sound`, `compositional_certificates_sound`,
  `certified_repair_preserves_meaning`, `certified_rewriting_cannot_repair_mistranslation`,
  `cachedCheck_sound`, `stale_cache_cannot_be_reused` (cache keyed by exact input bytes, no
  hash-collision assumption), `semantic_obligation_graph_acceptance_sound`,
  `wire_obligation_graph_acceptance_sound` (reuses the existing `ClaimGraphMemo` checker).

Receipt ledger (`ReceiptLedger.lean`)
* `acceptEnvelope_sound`, `replay_rejected`, `expired_rejected`, `revoked_rejected`,
  `role_mismatch_rejected`, `unknown_version_rejected`, `statement_substitution_rejected`,
  `envelopeBytes_injective`, `role_confusion_and_substitution_impossible` (under explicit
  `NoForgery` and issuer-honesty hypotheses).

Feedback (`SemanticFeedback.lean`)
* `label_positive_sound`, `label_negative_sound` — the only two *verified* training labels are
  semantically justified; `human_obligations_not_mechanical`.

Limits (`SemanticLimits.lean`) — results stating what PCS can **not** conclude
* `no_countermodel_found_within_bound_is_not_equivalence_proof` — the bounded search is exhausted
  at size 2 on inequivalent claims (a checked size-3 countermodel exists).
* `structural_rejection_is_not_semantic_inequivalence` — v1 structural rejection of claims that
  are equivalent in every model; v2 certifies them.
* `intent_gap` — for every candidate there is a possible intended meaning it does not express.
* `registry_name_does_not_fix_meaning` — symbol names do not determine meaning.

Intended Lean model (`SemanticV2LeanModel.lean`)
* `leanModel_conforms`; `*_lean_meaning` (PCS denotation in `leanModel` is literally the
  rendered Lean proposition); `{rev,repro,bio}_certified_lean_statements_equivalent` —
  kernel-checked equivalences between the actual Lean statements of the interpretation and the
  accepted candidate for the three certified fixtures. The example symbols are uninterpreted
  `opaque` placeholders; no domain fact is asserted.

## 3. What remains external (TCB)

* Lean compiler, runtime and file IO of `pcs-semantic-check` (executable-tested, not proved).
* Ed25519 unforgeability and key secrecy; honesty of the confirmation / elaboration / proof
  issuers (`ExternalContracts`, `NoForgery`).
* That the rendered Lean source elaborates to the claim's meaning in the intended model
  (`ElaborationBridge`); discharged inside Lean only for the fixture registry
  (`SemanticV2LeanModel.lean`). Source elaboration itself is checked executably
  (`tools/run_semantic_v2_end_to_end.sh`, `LeanEnvironmentTests`), not proved.
* Human confirmation is evidence of a selected interpretation, never proof of intent (`intent_gap`).
* Fixture receipts are signed with **public test keys**: they exercise verification, they are not
  evidence that any statement was kernel-proved.
* Python client and serializer (fail-closed, but not verified).

## 6. What PCS can truthfully say about a model-generated translation (v2)

If `pcs-semantic-check --v2` outputs `CERTIFIED_TRANSLATION` on raw input bytes, then
(kernel-checked, modulo compiler/IO): the bytes decode to a request satisfying every v1 gate,
and the selected structured interpretation and the candidate have **the same denotation in every
model of the approved symbols and under every valuation** — established either by normal-form
equality or by a replayed certificate of sound rewrite steps, whoever produced it. The decision
does not depend on proposer identity, confidence or self-reported confirmation. If it outputs
`VERIFIED_COUNTEREXAMPLE`, there is a checked registry-conforming finite model in which the two
claims differ, so the candidate is not a meaning-preserving translation.

## 7. What PCS still cannot truthfully claim

* that the selected interpretation is what the speaker meant (`intent_gap`), or that English
  (other than PCS-CNL) is semantically certified;
* that `SEARCH_EXHAUSTED` or a failed certificate search means inequivalence or equivalence
  (`no_countermodel_found_within_bound_is_not_equivalence_proof`);
* that a v1/structural rejection means the translation was wrong
  (`structural_rejection_is_not_semantic_inequivalence`);
* that symbol names fix meaning (`registry_name_does_not_fix_meaning`), or that Lean elaboration
  of arbitrary rendered text was proved inside Lean;
* that receipts signed with the fixture test keys show anything was kernel-proved;
* that proved assumptions hold in the world, or that the proposing model is correct.

## 8. Policies (changes relative to v1)

* **Equivalence policy:** acceptance requires full denotational equivalence (not entailment);
  certificates may only use the 34 catalogued classical equivalences, each proved sound.
* **Countermodel policy:** a countermodel is reported only after `checkCountermodel` re-checks it
  (registry conformance, inhabited sorts, differing truth values).
* **Feedback policy:** training labels `VERIFIED_POSITIVE` / `VERIFIED_SEMANTIC_NEGATIVE` only
  for proved outcomes; everything else is `UNRESOLVED` or `CONTRACT_REJECTION`.
* Ambiguity, grounding, confirmation, elaboration and proof-receipt policies are those of v1.
